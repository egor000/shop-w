# Shopping Assistance

Shopping assistance helps shoppers discover and understand products in an internet shop.

## Language

**Shopper**:
A person asking the assistant about products, whether or not they have a shop account.
_Avoid_: Account, customer account

**Product**:
A good offered by the shop that a shopper can discover, compare, or ask factual questions about.
_Avoid_: Goods, item

**Catalog**:
The shop's collection of products and their descriptive information, including categories.

**Department**:
A broad group of related product categories, such as Electronics or Outdoors.

**Category**:
A group of products of the same general kind within a department, such as Headphones. Each product belongs to one category.
_Avoid_: Product type (when referring to the catalog hierarchy)

**Rating**:
A product's aggregate score on a five-point scale together with its rating count. An unrated product has no average score.

**Product dimensions**:
A product's length, width, and height in its stated measurement configuration. These describe the product rather than its shipping package.

**Product weight**:
The weight of the product itself, excluding its shipping package.

**Conversation**:
A shopper's sequence of questions and assistant answers, with enough history to understand follow-up questions.
_Avoid_: Chat session

**Product discovery**:
Helping a shopper find products that match their stated needs.

**Product comparison**:
Explaining relevant differences between products using their recorded information.

**Shopper question**:
A single submission within a conversation seeking product information or clarification. Retrying the same submission does not create a new shopper question.
_Avoid_: Query, message (when referring specifically to the shopper's submission)

**Product answer**:
The assistant's authoritative completed response to a shopper question, grounded in the catalog information available to it.
